# Notes for AI assistants

- **Asked to deploy or host Rondel** (in AWS, Azure, Google Cloud, Kubernetes, a company server): follow
  [DEPLOY-WITH-AI.md](DEPLOY-WITH-AI.md). [DEPLOY.md](DEPLOY.md) explains every setting.
- **Asked to change the code:** follow [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md). Run `npm test` before you finish, and
  sign off every commit (`git commit -s`).
- **Asked to write a meeting template:** see [TEMPLATES.md](TEMPLATES.md).
- Never put a meeting link in an issue, log or commit: the part after `#` is the key to the meeting.
